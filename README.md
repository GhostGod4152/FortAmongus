# FortAmongus: Battle Bus Among Us

> **Status: design draft. There is no playable build yet.**

A BepInEx 6 (IL2CPP) mod for **Among Us**. Everyone drops from a Battle Bus onto the map, grabs loot, does tasks and survives a shrinking storm while impostors hunt.

## Required games
- **Among Us** (host, installed through Melty with BepInEx 6 IL2CPP)
- **Fortnite** (companion: its rules are used; no Fortnite files are shipped)

## Planned features
- Battle Bus drop at round start with a chosen jump spot
- Shrinking storm that pushes everyone together
- Loot pickups: weapons for impostors, healing for everyone
- Impostor kills with loot weapons, plus normal tasks and meetings
- Multiplayer, target 10 players with 2 impostors (not yet tested)
